## GeoTrainer

# GeoTrainer — Full Edition

Map games and geography concepts in one collection: world countries, ten country-subdivision packs, physical geography, plate tectonics, islands and archipelagos, reference lines and time.

**How to study:** Start with one subject or task subdeck, then add others as useful. Current focused components share the same notes as this full edition. Games provide feedback; you choose Anki’s grade yourself.

**Read more:** [Geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography) · [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification)
