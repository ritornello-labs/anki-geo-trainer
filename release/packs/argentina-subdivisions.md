Learn the shapes and locations of **Argentina’s 23 provinces and the autonomous city of Buenos Aires** through four spatial-recall games: **96 notes / 96 cards**, covering all 24 first-level jurisdictions.

## Identify a jurisdiction

Name the jurisdiction containing the dot on a map without internal borders. Reveal its name and outline.

![Identify Córdoba in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-05-v1/geo-trainer-argentina-subdivisions/point.gif)

## Place the shape

Drag Córdoba's silhouette into position on the blank map, then reveal the answer and native distance feedback.

![Place Córdoba in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-05-v1/geo-trainer-argentina-subdivisions/place.gif)

## Sketch in context

Draw Córdoba where it belongs on the map. The answer compares your outline with its shape and position.

![Sketch Córdoba in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-05-v1/geo-trainer-argentina-subdivisions/sketch.gif)

## Draw from memory

Recall Córdoba's outline on a square canvas without a map or aspect-ratio hint. The answer compares shape independently of drawing size and position.

![Draw Córdoba in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-05-v1/geo-trainer-argentina-subdivisions/draw.gif)

## Using the pack

There are **24 identification, 24 placement, 24 contextual sketch and 24 outline cards**, in four task subdecks. Every jurisdiction has all four exercises. Buenos Aires province and the autonomous city are separate entities; the city is labeled **Ciudad de Buenos Aires**.

Games show feedback and a suggested grade; **you choose Anki's grade yourself**. Identification uses ordinary recall. Geometry and game code are bundled for offline study.

This component will also be included in [GeoTrainer's full edition](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/908455862), whose update ships after the components. Shared scopes retain the same model IDs, leaf deck IDs and note GUIDs, so overlapping imports update the same notes.

## Source edition

Geometry uses [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) public-domain administrative data with the project's own projection. This is a simplified map exercise rather than a detailed boundary survey. Argentina's [official territorial catalog](https://datos.gob.ar/dataset/ign-unidades-territoriales/archivo/ign_01.02.01) distinguishes the 23 provinces and autonomous city. The scope, map edition and reproducible build are recorded in the repository.
