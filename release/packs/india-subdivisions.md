Learn the shapes and locations of India's **36 states and union territories** through four spatial-recall games: **140 notes / 140 cards**.

## Identify a subdivision

Name the unit containing the dot on a map without internal borders. Reveal the answer to see its name and outline.

![Identify Maharashtra in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-india-subdivisions/point.gif)

## Place the shape

Drag Maharashtra's silhouette into position on a blank map, then reveal the answer to compare its location with native distance feedback.

![Place Maharashtra in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-india-subdivisions/place.gif)

## Sketch in context

Draw Maharashtra where it belongs on the map. The answer compares your outline with its real shape and position.

![Sketch Maharashtra in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-india-subdivisions/sketch.gif)

## Draw from memory

Recall Maharashtra's outline on a square canvas without a map or an aspect-ratio hint. The answer compares shape independently of drawing size and position.

![Draw Maharashtra's outline in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-india-subdivisions/draw.gif)

## Using the pack

There are **36 identification, 36 placement, 32 contextual sketch and 36 outline cards**, in four task subdecks. Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Lakshadweep and Puducherry are omitted from contextual sketching because their footprints are small; all four retain the other three exercises. Ladakh and Jammu and Kashmir are separate units, and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu is one merged unit.

Games give feedback and a suggested grade; **you choose Anki's grade yourself**. Identification uses ordinary recall. Geometry and game code are bundled for offline study.

This component will also be included in [GeoTrainer's full edition](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/908455862), whose update ships after the components. Shared scopes retain the same model IDs, leaf deck IDs and note GUIDs, so overlapping imports update the same notes.

## Sources and edition

Geometry uses [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) public-domain administrative data with the project's own projection and reconciled subdivision names and boundaries. Boundaries follow the source dataset and are simplified for recall; small and detached areas may be hard to distinguish at this scale. The map is a learning reference. Source details and licenses are recorded in the repository.
