Learn the **26 Brazilian states and the Federal District** through four kinds of spatial recall: identify a state from a dot, drag its silhouette into place, sketch it on a blank Brazil map, and draw its outline from memory.

**108 cards — 27 federative units × 4 tasks.** The Federal District appears alongside the states in each task subdeck.

## 1. Identify the state

A dot appears inside a state on a map without internal borders. Recall its name, then reveal the highlighted answer. The dot changes between reviews.

![Identify Bahia from a dot on the borderless Brazil map, then reveal the answer](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-03-v2/geo-trainer-brazil-states/point.gif)

## 2. Place the silhouette

Drag the supplied state shape to its geographic position. The answer compares your placement with the correct location.

![Drag Bahia into place and reveal the placement feedback](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-03-v2/geo-trainer-brazil-states/place.gif)

## 3. Sketch it on the map

Draw the named state in position on a blank Brazil map. The answer overlays the true outline and checks shape, position and size.

![Sketch Bahia on the blank Brazil map and compare the result with its true outline](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-03-v2/geo-trainer-brazil-states/sketch.gif)

## 4. Draw its outline from memory

Draw on a blank square canvas without a map to guide you. The answer aligns your drawing with the true outline for comparison.

![Draw Bahia from memory on a blank canvas and reveal the outline comparison](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-03-v2/geo-trainer-brazil-states/draw.gif)

## Studying with GeoTrainer

The four tasks live in separate subdecks, so you can start with identification and placement before adding sketching and drawing. The cards give feedback; **you choose Anki’s answer grade**. Study offline on Anki Desktop, AnkiMobile or AnkiDroid, with light/dark styling and zoom/pan controls for drawing tasks.

This component will also be included in [GeoTrainer’s full edition](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/908455862), whose update will ship after the components. Shared content keeps the same note GUIDs, note-type IDs and leaf deck IDs so overlapping imports update the same notes.

## Source & issues

Subdivision geometry comes from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), which provides public-domain map data.
