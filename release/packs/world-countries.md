Learn country shapes, locations, regional groupings and international organizations through map games and focused recall: **900 notes / 1,220 cards**.

## Identify a country

Name the country containing the dot on a map without internal borders. Reveal the answer to see its outline and name. The geometry scopes cover 195 countries across Europe, Africa, Asia, North America, South America and Oceania.

![Identify Italy in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-world-countries/point.gif)

## Place the shape

Drag Italy's silhouette into position on a blank regional map, then compare your placement with the answer and its distance feedback.

![Place Italy in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-world-countries/place.gif)

## Sketch in context

Draw France where it belongs on a blank map. The answer compares your outline with the country's real shape and position.

![Sketch France in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-world-countries/sketch.gif)

## Draw from memory

Recall Italy's outline on a square canvas without a map or aspect-ratio hint. The answer compares shape independently of drawing size and position. Continent silhouettes have both drawing and contextual sketching cards too.

![Draw Italy's outline in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-world-countries/draw.gif)

Together these tasks make **765 geometry cards**: 195 identification, 195 placement, 168 country sketches, 195 country outlines, and 12 continent sketch/outline cards.

## Select the members

The **59 membership drills** ask you to click the countries that belong to a bloc or grouping. Zoom and pan to reach small countries; reveal the answer to see selected and missed members.

![Select bloc members in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-world-countries/membership.gif)

## Recall the concepts

The **396 concept cards from 76 notes** cover 13 regional groupings and 63 country blocs or organizations. Recall names, locations, members and the basis for regional groupings; connect organizations with their acronyms, founding dates, purposes and membership sizes. Conditional cards appear where the corresponding information is available.

![Recall a regional grouping in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-world-countries/concept.gif)

## Using the pack

Games give feedback and a suggested grade; **you choose Anki's grade yourself**. Identification and concept cards use ordinary recall. Required geometry, game code and all 77 concept maps are bundled for offline study; embedded Wikipedia references need an internet connection.

This component will also be included in [GeoTrainer's full edition](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/908455862), whose update ships after the components. Shared scopes retain the same model IDs, leaf deck IDs and note GUIDs, so overlapping imports update the same notes.

## Sources and edition

Maps and geometry use [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) public-domain data, with locally generated outlines and locators. Borders, country coverage and regional divisions follow the source datasets and are learning references. Concept notes retain their Wikipedia references and applicable CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution. The concept sources are the accepted September 2026 edition; memberships and geopolitical facts can change, so consult the linked references for current details. Regional groupings can have fuzzy or disputed membership, explained where relevant on their cards. Source details and licenses are recorded in the repository.
