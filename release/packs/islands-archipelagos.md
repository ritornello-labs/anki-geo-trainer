Locate islands and archipelagos on a globe, then connect their positions with regional geography: **76 notes / 112 cards**.

## Rotate, mark and refine

Rotate a randomly oriented globe to find Japan from memory. Draw an ellipse across its extent; drag inside to move it and use the handles to resize. Reveal the answer to compare your estimate with the highlighted islands, coverage and center offset.

![Rotate the globe and mark Japan's extent in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-islands-archipelagos/globe.gif)

The **67 globe-placement cards** cover island countries and territories across the Pacific, Caribbean, Atlantic, Indian Ocean and Mediterranean. The front shows continents and large island landmasses as unlabeled anchors, plus the equator and tropics; small islands are omitted. On the answer, all deck islands appear, with the target highlighted and labels where they fit. You can keep rotating the answer globe or focus it on the target.

## Recall archipelago geography

The **45 concept cards from nine notes** cover the Malay Archipelago, British Isles, Japanese archipelago, Aleutian Islands, Kuril Islands, Canary Islands, Balearic Islands, Greater Antilles and Lesser Antilles. Recall names from definitions, locate the archipelagos, and recall countries, surrounding waters and distinctive features.

![Recall the Japanese archipelago's surrounding waters in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-islands-archipelagos/concept.gif)

## Using the pack

Globe games show a suggested grade; **you choose Anki's grade yourself**. Concept cards use ordinary recall. Globe geometry, game code and all ten concept maps are bundled for offline study; embedded Wikipedia references need an internet connection. The pack has two task/concept subdecks and two note types.

This component will also be included in [GeoTrainer's full edition](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/908455862), whose update ships after the components. Shared scopes retain the same model IDs, leaf deck IDs and note GUIDs, so overlapping imports update the same notes.

## Sources and edition

Globe geometry and concept maps use [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) public-domain data, with locally generated outlines and locators. Geometry uses Natural Earth's 1:110m, 1:50m and 1:10m datasets; small islands and complex archipelagos are simplified for recall. The globe runtime includes [D3](https://github.com/d3/d3-geo) under its ISC license. Concept notes link their Wikipedia references; Wikipedia-derived prose retains CC BY-SA 4.0 where applicable. Source details and original licenses are recorded in the repository.
