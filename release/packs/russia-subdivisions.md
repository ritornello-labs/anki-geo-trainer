## Using the pack

There are **85 identification, 85 placement, 78 contextual sketch and 85 outline cards**, in four task subdecks. Republic of Adygea, Republic of Ingushetia, Karelia, Moscow (city), Republic of North Ossetia-Alania, Saint Petersburg and Sevastopol omit contextual sketching under the existing geometry-selection rule; all seven retain the other three exercises.

Games show feedback and a suggested grade; **you choose Anki's grade yourself**. Identification uses ordinary recall. Geometry and game code are bundled for offline study.

This component will also be included in [GeoTrainer's full edition](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/908455862), whose update ships after the components. Shared scopes retain the same model IDs, leaf deck IDs and note GUIDs, so overlapping imports update the same notes.

## Territory and source edition

Geometry uses [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) public-domain administrative data with the project's own projection. This dataset's 85-unit map includes **Crimea and Sevastopol, internationally recognized as parts of Ukraine**, as described by the [UN General Assembly](https://press.un.org/en/2014/ga11493.doc.htm). Their inclusion documents the source map's coverage; the deck does not present them as internationally recognized Russian territory. This pack is a source-edition geography exercise, with simplified boundaries, rather than a current legal administrative register.

English labels correct malformed and older short names, including Magadan and Zabaykalsky Krai, and distinguish Moscow city from Moscow Oblast and Altai Krai from the Altai Republic. Existing note keys and geometry remain stable. Source-label reconciliation and the full scope are recorded in the repository.
