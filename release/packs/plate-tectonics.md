Learn the shapes, positions and relationships of tectonic plates through map games and focused recall: **204 notes / 492 cards**.

## Identify a plate

A dot marks a location inside a plate. Recall its name, then reveal the plate outline. The dot changes position between reviews.

![Identify the African Plate in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-plate-tectonics/point.gif)

## Place a plate

Drag the Arabian Plate into position, then compare its placement with the answer. Placement games include 16 mapped plates and 35 minor plates.

![Place the Arabian Plate in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-plate-tectonics/place.gif)

## Sketch a plate

Sketch the Arabian Plate on a blank map, then compare its position and shape with the answer. Zoom and pan for smaller regions. Sketch games cover the 16 mapped plates and 32 minor plates with suitable outlines.

![Sketch the Arabian Plate in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-plate-tectonics/sketch.gif)

## Trace a boundary

Zoom and pan toward California, trace the San Andreas Fault, then reveal its course and compare your line. Trace cards cover 17 named plate boundaries, including ridges, rifts, trenches and faults.

![Zoom, pan and trace the San Andreas Fault in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-plate-tectonics/trace.gif)

## Connect geography with geology

The **360 concept cards from 72 notes** cover four accepted families: 17 plate concepts; 35 minor plates; convergent, divergent and transform boundary types; and 17 named boundaries. Recall names and locations, plate kinds and what they carry, boundary motion and effects, and which plates meet at named boundaries.

![Recall the San Andreas Fault boundary type in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-plate-tectonics/concept.gif)

## Using the pack

The 132 map-game cards give feedback and a suggested grade; **you choose Anki's grade yourself**. Concept cards use ordinary recall. Maps and game code are bundled for offline study; embedded Wikipedia references need an internet connection. The pack has ten task/concept subdecks and ten note types, with all 70 required media files included.

This component will also be included in [GeoTrainer's full edition](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/908455862), whose update ships after the components. Shared scopes retain the same model IDs, leaf deck IDs and note GUIDs, so overlapping imports update the same notes.

## Sources and edition

Plate and boundary geometry uses [Peter Bird's PB2002 model, converted by Hugo Ahlenius / Nordpil](https://github.com/fraxen/tectonicplates), under the [Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). Context maps use [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) public-domain data. Concept locator maps use [TUBS's equirectangular world location map](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:World_location_map_(equirectangular_180).svg), CC BY-SA 3.0, with selected plates or boundary traces overlaid. The repository records source links and modifications; original media licenses apply. Plate outlines follow the referenced model and simplify complex deformation zones for recall.
