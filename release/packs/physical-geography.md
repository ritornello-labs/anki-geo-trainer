Learn physical geography through map games and focused recall: **404 notes / 963 cards**. Trace rivers, place landmarks, sketch regions, and connect landscapes with their climate and vegetation.

## Trace a river

Zoom and pan a blank map, then trace the Amazon's course. Reveal the answer to compare your line with the real river and see feedback.

![Trace the Amazon in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-physical-geography/river.gif)

## Place a landscape

Drag the Sahara into position. Placement cards also cover lakes, mountain ranges, plateaus, grasslands and peninsulas; lake cards include an identification exercise.

![Place the Sahara in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-physical-geography/place.gif)

## Sketch a region

Sketch the Tibetan Plateau on a blank map, then compare its position and shape with the answer. Sketch cards cover ranges, deserts, plateaus, grasslands and peninsulas.

![Sketch the Tibetan Plateau in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-physical-geography/sketch.gif)

## Recall the concepts

The **713 concept cards** cover peninsulas; straits, canals and isthmuses; capes; ranges; deserts; plateaus, highlands and basins; plains, grasslands and steppes; biomes; and the Köppen class and letter systems. Name and Locate cards stay alongside the topic-specific questions.

Biome cards connect distribution, vegetation and climate, with representative photos and their credits.

![Recall tropical moist forest vegetation in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-physical-geography/biome.gif)

The Köppen cards include **21 selected class/group maps** from the 1991–2020 classification, plus 19 letter-system notes. Recall codes, names, criteria, examples and the meaning of the letters.

![Identify the Af tropical rainforest distribution in real Anki](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-physical-geography/koppen.gif)

## Using the pack

The 250 map-game cards give feedback and a suggested grade; **you choose Anki's grade yourself**. The concept cards use ordinary recall. Required maps, photos and game code are bundled for offline study; embedded Wikipedia references need an internet connection.

This component will also be included in [GeoTrainer's full edition](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/908455862), whose update ships after the components. Shared scopes retain the same model IDs, leaf deck IDs and note GUIDs, so overlapping imports update the same notes.

## Sources and edition

Maps use [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) public-domain data. Biome distributions use [RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017](https://ecoregions.world/) (CC BY 4.0); biome photos retain individual author, source and license links on their cards. Köppen maps use [Beck and colleagues' Köppen–Geiger v2 maps](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02549-6) (CC BY 4.0), via Wikimedia Commons, cropped to remove answer-revealing legends; polar overlays are recoloured for visibility. Detailed media credits and source links are in the repository. Boundaries and climate regions are learning references; real landscapes have transition zones.
