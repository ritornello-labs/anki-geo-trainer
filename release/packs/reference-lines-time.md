Learn the lines that organize the globe and the rules that organize the clock. Rotate a globe to place parallels and meridians, recall why the tropics and polar circles matter, reason about calendar dates, and practice time-zone offsets.

**78 cards from 56 notes:** 24 reference-line and time drills, plus 54 atlas cards from 32 place notes. The atlas includes standard/daylight offsets where specified and 12 selected abbreviation cards.

## 1. Place a reference line on the globe

Drag to rotate, then tap to place the requested parallel or meridian. Reveal the correct line and its latitude or longitude. Includes the Equator, Greenwich meridian, both tropics, both polar circles and the 180° meridian.

![Rotate the globe, place the Tropic of Cancer, and reveal its 23.5-degree north parallel](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-reference-lines-time/globe.gif)

## 2. Convert UTC and the calendar date

Practice randomized conversions between UTC and UTC−3 in both directions. Give the hour and whether the result is on the previous, same or next day.

![Convert 22:00 in UTC minus three to 01:00 UTC on the next day](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-reference-lines-time/utc.gif)

## 3. Understand the reference lines

Recall latitude and longitude, axial tilt, tropical overhead Sun, polar day/night, the International Date Line, and UTC foundations. Illustrated answers explain the relevant geometry. The date-line cards distinguish the real calendar boundary from the straight 180° meridian.

![Recall the date adjustment when crossing the International Date Line and reveal the illustrated answer](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-reference-lines-time/date-line.gif)

## 4. Build a time-zone atlas

Recall a named place’s UTC offset, then reveal its zone name and a map showing the place and offset region. Includes whole-hour, half-hour and quarter-hour offsets across six continents. Seasonal cards state the standard/daylight condition; selected abbreviations have their own cards.

![Recall Kathmandu's UTC offset and reveal Nepal Time and the map](https://ritornello.dev/media/ankiweb/2026-10-04-v1/geo-trainer-reference-lines-time/atlas.gif)

## Studying with GeoTrainer

The ten leaf subdecks separate coordinates, tropics/polar circles, the date line, UTC foundations and the six atlas continents. Start with the foundations and add atlas practice as useful. Required maps and code are bundled for offline study. **You choose Anki’s answer grade.**

This component will also be included in [GeoTrainer’s full edition](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/908455862), whose update will ship after the components. Shared content keeps the same note GUIDs, note-type IDs and leaf deck IDs so overlapping imports update the same notes.
